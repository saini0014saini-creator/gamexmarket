No. GameXMarket exclusively uses irreversible Indian domestic payment channels (UPI and IMPS Bank Transfer). Unlike PayPal or international cards which suffer from fake unauthorized dispute scams, payments deposited in GameX escrow cannot be reversed once verified.

When and how does the 5% platform fee get deducted? expand_more

There are absolutely zero upfront fees to list an account. The 5% commission is automatically calculated and deducted only when a successful sale concludes and funds are remitted to your registered UPI ID or Bank account.

What happens if the buyer takes too long to confirm delivery? expand_more

Buyers have a 2-hour window upon credential transmission to verify login credentials and in-game specifications. If no dispute is filed within this grace period, our system auto-finalizes the transaction and immediately releases the net payout to your UPI.
